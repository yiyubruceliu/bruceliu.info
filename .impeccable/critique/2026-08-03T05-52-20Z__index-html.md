#### Priority Issues

**[P0] Mobile section nav missing**
- **What:** At 390px, `#nav-menu-container` is hidden and `#mobile-nav-toggle` / `#mobile-nav` are absent from the DOM.
- **Why it matters:** ~9800px page with no mobile IA is a scroll trap.
- **Fix:** Always inject/show hamburger; sticky jump targets; CSS-only fallback if JS flaky.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt` then `/impeccable harden`

**[P0] Recommendations carousel is empty**
- **What:** `.testi_slider.owl-carousel` stays `display:none`, owl-item count 0 — testimonials in markup never surface.
- **Why it matters:** Peak-end valley; social proof is a broken promise.
- **Fix:** Static/stack of 2–3 curated quotes; kill owl autoplay dependency.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden` + `/impeccable distill`

**[P1] Mobile hero is a wall of social pills**
- **What:** Five full-width social pills before “Let's chat”; fixed share strip also in thumb zone.
- **Why it matters:** Primary conversion loses to secondary profile tourism.
- **Fix:** One primary CTA + one secondary (LinkedIn/CV); demote other socials; thin/remove mobile share strip.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill` + `/impeccable quieter`

**[P1] Template DNA overpowers EdTech identity**
- **What:** Skill % bars, Steve Jobs quote, 20-logo grid, decorative geometry, Roboto/Poppins, Uzumaki section rhythm. Detector agrees on nested-card / hairline+shadow chrome density.
- **Why it matters:** Interchangeable = forgettable.
- **Fix:** Cut skill meters and logo spam; keep 4–6 proof logos; lead with one artifact; typeset for education/tech character.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable shape` or `/impeccable bolder` · `/impeccable typeset`

**[P2] Contact strip looks actionable but isn't**
- **What:** Phone/email are non-link `<h5>`s; share intents omit absolute HTTPS URLs.
- **Why it matters:** Can't tap-to-contact; share may fail.
- **Fix:** `tel:` / `mailto:` / `wa.me`; fix share URLs; ≥44px hit targets.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify` + `/impeccable harden`
